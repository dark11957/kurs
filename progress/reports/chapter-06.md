## Grammar covered

1. Common prepositions with accusative: durch, für, gegen, ohne, um, bis and entlang.
2. Common prepositions with dative: aus, außer, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu, gegenüber, ab and entgegen.
3. The nine two-way prepositions: an, auf, hinter, in, neben, über, unter, vor and zwischen.
4. Wo? for location and Wohin? for a destination with two-way prepositions; fixed-case prepositions retain their case.
5. Location, direction and origin expressions, including cities, countries, zu, aus/von, zu Hause and nach Hause.
6. Position and placement verb pairs: stehen/stellen, liegen/legen, sitzen/setzen and the two uses of hängen.
7. Common preposition–article contractions and the case they preserve.

Seven grammar rules each include an explanation, pattern, examples, exceptions, common error, memory tip and three mini-exercises. Tables and comparisons cover article forms, fixed preposition groups, contractions and frequently confused directions.
